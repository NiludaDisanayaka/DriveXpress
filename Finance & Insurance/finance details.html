Finance Overview

Our comprehensive vehicle finance solutions connect you with Sri Lanka's leading banks and financial institutions, offering tailored options for both new and pre-owned vehicles.

Available Finance Options

Choose from our diverse range of financing plans designed to suit different financial profiles and requirements.

Bank Vehicle Loans

  • Suitable for new and used vehicles
  • Fixed or reducing balance interest options
  • Repayment periods up to several years
  • Competitive interest rates based on profile

Leasing Facilities

  • Vehicle ownership after final payment
  • Flexible monthly rental plans
  • Lower initial cash requirement
  • Ideal for salaried and self-employed customers

Dealer-Assisted Finance

  • Quick processing through partner institutions
  • Guidance on documentation and approvals
  • Convenient one-stop financing support

Eligibility Requirements

We've streamlined our eligibility criteria to make vehicle financing accessible to a wide range of customers.

Basic Requirements

  • Sri Lankan citizen with valid identification
  • Age typically between 18 and retirement age
  • Stable income from employment or business
  • Acceptable credit history and repayment capacity

Required Documents

  • National Identity Card or Passport
  • Proof of income (salary slips or bank statements)
  • Vehicle quotation or proforma invoice
  • Proof of address and employment details

Financial Terms & Conditions

Transparent financial terms designed to help you make informed decisions.
